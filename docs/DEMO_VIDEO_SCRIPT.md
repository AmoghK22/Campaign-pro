# 3–5 minute demo video script

## 0:00–0:20 — Explain the product
Open Home. Say: "This platform helps people pool money to amplify messages beyond the creator's existing network. Contributions pay for distribution, not directly for the cause. Reach numbers are estimates."

## 0:20–0:55 — Discover
Search for a campaign, filter by category, open a detail page, scroll through the story and sources, expand use of funds, and show estimated reach/funding progress.

## 0:55–1:55 — Create
Tap Create. Show the five-step progress indicator. Fill Basics, add a story section and source, set an ad headline/visual, choose audience/location/channel/duration, and set the funding goal at Submit. Save a draft and briefly show that it can be resumed. Submit and show the review timeline.

## 1:55–2:45 — Back a campaign
Open a campaign, choose a contribution, show the live fee/total/estimated-impressions calculation, and read the amplification disclaimer. Use the clearly labelled simulated successful payment. Show receipt and updated progress. If time permits, demonstrate a failed or pending outcome without increasing funding.

## 2:45–3:35 — Track
Open Activity and Profile. Show contribution history and creator dashboard. Open the distribution report and distinguish funds raised, advertising spend, impressions, and estimated/measured metrics.

## 3:35–4:00 — Close
Summarize the key product rules: browse without login, drafts persist, campaigns are reviewed, contributions fund distribution, and funding does not automatically launch ads.

## Recording tips
- Use seeded data that has enough funding progress to look credible.
- Keep personal notifications, email, and private account information off-screen.
- Do not claim real payment, live advertising, real audience delivery, or external verification.
- Record a clean run after restarting the app once to prove persistence.
